# Product Terminology

Status: repository-owned operational policy.

This document is the terminology reference for current planning, architecture,
OpenSpec, and cross-repository coordination work. It makes the approved
language available to contributors and LLM agents without requiring them to
interpret the JLP source corpus for every change.

## Authority and use

The JLP [Lexicon](../ai-planning/jpaul-documents/markdown/Reinvest-to-Grow%E2%84%A2%20Methodology/Supporting%20Documents/Lexicon.md)
is the source material for the conceptual definitions. This document applies
those definitions to repository, product, and implementation language.

When a source document conflicts with this policy, preserve historical or
quoted source language and raise the conflict for explicit human resolution.
Do not silently redefine Theory, Methodology, Framework, System, Platform, or
App as interchangeable terms.

## Naming rules

- The approved standalone public display name is **`Reinvest-to-Grow™`**.
- Use lowercase hyphenated forms for technical slugs and identifiers, such as
  `reinvest-to-grow`. Do not put the trademark glyph in machine identifiers.
- Use the trademarked display form in current human-facing copy. Preserve old
  spellings in historical evidence, quotations, and migration records when
  changing them would rewrite what happened.
- Use the semantic qualifier when the text refers to a specific concept, such
  as **Reinvest-to-Grow™ Methodology** or **Reinvest-to-Grow™ Theory**.

## Controlled terms

| Term | Refers to | Use it for | Do not use it as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Reinvest-to-Grow™** | The named HRF enterprise-development initiative and brand | Standalone public references to the named initiative | A replacement for every Theory, Methodology, Framework, or product term |
| **Reinvest-to-Grow™ Theory** | The evidence-based explanation of enterprise transformation and its testable hypotheses | Conceptual and research claims about why enterprise transformation occurs | The implementation process or software platform |
| **Reinvest-to-Grow™ Methodology** | The practical implementation of the Theory through coordinated interventions | Operating practice, delivery design, and methodology-specific requirements | The entire software product or physical operating business |
| **Reinvest-to-Grow™ Framework** | The conceptual organizing structure used to relate mechanisms and outcomes | Models, diagrams, and analytical structures | A synonym for the deployed application |
| **Enterprise Growth System** | The integrated operating whole of enterprise-development capabilities and practices | System-level business and operating discussions | The mobile app or platform alone |
| **Enterprise Growth Platform** | The integrated operational infrastructure coordinating purchasing, financing, enterprise growth services, measurement, and digital data | Platform architecture and cross-component capability | A synonym for one application |
| **Enterprise Growth App** | The entrepreneur-facing application that is a component of the Platform | Mobile or entrepreneur-facing product requirements and UI | `Recipient App` or `Entrepreneur Application` as current product names |
| **Business Journal** | The Version 1 record and insight module within the Enterprise Growth App | The initial business-activity and bookkeeping-oriented module | The entire Enterprise Growth App or Platform |
| **Enterprise Supply Hub** | The physical operating business for purchasing, supply access, service delivery, and validation | Physical operations, unit economics, and supply workflows | The digital platform |
| **HRF Administrative Portal** | The staff-facing administrative product | Staff workflows, administration, and nonprofit operations | `Nonprofit Back Office` or `Staff Web` as current product names |
| **Home Roots Foundation (HRF)** | The organization owning and operating the initiative | First formal organizational reference, then `HRF` | A product or repository name |

## Legacy and context-specific terms

- **Invest in Growth** is legacy wording in existing planning and bootstrap
  records. Do not introduce it into new current product copy without an
  explicit decision that it is a separate approved program name.
- **Recipient**, **microloan**, and **microlending** may remain when describing
  a specific historical source, lending product, vendor category, or quoted
  program context. They are not the default names for the current platform or
  primary user role.
- Historical filenames, archived OpenSpec evidence, quotations, and source
  transcripts retain their original wording unless a migration note or an
  explicitly approved rename requires otherwise.

## Change discipline

Before introducing a new product or program name, check this policy and the
JLP Lexicon, state which existing term it replaces or complements, and obtain
human approval for any material semantic change. Update this policy first when
an approved terminology decision should govern future repository work.
